Theorem 31.

(The negatives.) Let \(R\) be a ring, and let \(x,y,z \in R\text{.}\) Then
🔗
  • \(\displaystyle -(-x) = x\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • \(\displaystyle x(-y)=-(xy) = (-x)y\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • \(\displaystyle (-x)(-y) = xy\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • \(\displaystyle x(y-z) = xy-xz\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • \(\displaystyle (y-z)x= yx-zx\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • \(\displaystyle -(x+y)=-x-y\)
