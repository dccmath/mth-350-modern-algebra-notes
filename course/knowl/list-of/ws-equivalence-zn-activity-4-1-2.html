Definition 11 Equivalence class.

Let \(\sim\) be an equivalence relation on a nonempty set \(S\text{,}\) and let \(a\in S\text{.}\) The equivalence class of \(a\) (with respect to \(\sim\) ) denoted \([a]_{\sim}\) is the set of all elements of \(S\) that are related to \(a\) by \(\sim\text{.}\) More precisely,
\begin{equation*} [a]_{\sim}= \{ x \in S: x\sim a\}. \end{equation*}
