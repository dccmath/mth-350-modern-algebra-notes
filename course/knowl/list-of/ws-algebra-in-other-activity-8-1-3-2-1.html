Definition 40.

A complex number is any number of the form \(a+bi\) where \(a,b\in \mathbb{R}\) and \(i\) is an imaginary number with the property that \(i^{2} = -1\text{.}\) For a complex number \(x=a+bi\text{,}\) the real number is called the real part of \(x\) and \(b\) is called the imaginary part of \(x\text{.}\) So
\begin{equation*} \mathbb{C}= \{a+bi: a\in \mathbb{R}, b\in\mathbb{R}, \text{ and }i^{2} = -1\}. \end{equation*}
Furthermore, addition and multiplication within \(\mathbb{C}\) are defined by
\begin{equation*} (a+bi) + (c+di) = (a+c) + (b+d)i \end{equation*}
and
\begin{equation*} (a+bi)(c+di) = (ac - bd) + (ad+bc)i. \end{equation*}
