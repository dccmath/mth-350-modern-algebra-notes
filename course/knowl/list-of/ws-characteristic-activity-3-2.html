Definition 65 Addition and multiplication in rings.

Let \(R\) be a ring and let \(x\in R\text{.}\) For every \(n\in\mathbb{N}\) we define
  • \(\displaystyle nx = \underbrace{x+x +\cdots + x}_{n \text{ terms }}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • \(\displaystyle x^{n} = \underbrace{x\cdot x \cdots x}_{n \text{ factors}}\)
