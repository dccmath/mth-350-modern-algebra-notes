Theorem 32.

(Generalized Distributive Law) Let \(R\) be a ring, let \(n\ge 2\text{,}\) and let \(a,b_{1},b_{2},\dots, b_{n}\in R\text{.}\) Then
  • \(\displaystyle a(b_{1}+b_{2}+\cdots+b_{n})=ab_{1}+ab_{2}+\cdots+ab_{n}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • \(\displaystyle (b_{1}+b_{2}+\cdots+b_{n})a = b_{1}a+b_{2}a+\cdots+b_{n}a.\)
