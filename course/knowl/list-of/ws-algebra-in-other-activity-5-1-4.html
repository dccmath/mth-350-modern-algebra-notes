Definition 39 Multiplying polynomials.

Multiplying Polynomials: The product of polynomials \(p(x)\) (of degree \(m\)) and \(q(x)\) (of degree \(n\)) is defined to be
\begin{equation*} p(x)q(x) = c_{m+n}x^{m+n}+ c_{m+n-1}x^{m+n-1}+ \cdots + c_{2}x^{2} + c_{1}x + c_{0}. \end{equation*}
where for each \(k\) with \(0\le k \le m+n\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} c_{k} = a_{k}b_{0} + a_{k-1}b_{1} + a_{k-2}b_{2} + \cdots + a_{2}b_{k-2}+ a_{1}b_{k-1}+a_{0}b_{k}. \end{equation*}
