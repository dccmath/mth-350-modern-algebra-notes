Theorem 63 The Rational Root Theorem.

Let
\begin{equation*} f(x) = a_{n}x^{n} + a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+ \cdots +a_{1}x + a_{0}\in \mathbb{Z}[x] \end{equation*}
with \(a_{n}\neq 0\text{.}\) If \(r = \frac{p}{q}\in \mathbb{Q}\) is a root of \(f(x)\) with \(\gcd(p,q)=1\) then \(p\mid a_{0}\) and \(q\mid a_{n}\text{.}\)
