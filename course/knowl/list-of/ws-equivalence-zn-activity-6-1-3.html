Definition 14 \(\mathbb{Z}_{n}\).

For every integer \(n\ge 2\text{,}\) the integers modulo \(n\) denoted \(\mathbb{Z}_{n}\text{,}\) is the set of the distinct congruence classes of \(\Z\) modulo \(n\text{,}\) i.e.,
\begin{equation*} \Z_{n} = \{[0]_{n}, [1]_{n}, [2]_{n}, \dots, [n-1]_{n}\}. \end{equation*}
