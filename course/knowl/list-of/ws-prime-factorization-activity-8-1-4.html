Definition 58 Irreducible polynomial.

For an integral domain \(D\text{,}\) a polynomial \(f(x)\) from \(D[x]\) that is neither the zero polynomial nor a unit in \(D[x]\) is said to be irreducible over \(D\) if whenever \(f(x)\) is expressed as a product \(f(x)=g(x)h(x)\) with \(g(x)\) and \(h(x)\) from \(D[x]\text{,}\) then \(g(x)\) or \(h(x)\) is a unit in \(D[x]\text{.}\) A nonzero, nonunit element of \(D[x]\) that is not irreducible over \(D\) is called reducible.
